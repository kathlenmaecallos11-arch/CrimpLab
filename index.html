What is crimping?

Crimping presses a connector onto the end of a cable so it holds firmly and makes electrical contact.

Cat5 cable

Category 5 cable has four twisted pairs of copper wire (eight wires) inside one jacket. The twists reduce interference. Cat5 supports 100 Mbps Ethernet over up to 100 meters. Cat5e is the improved version and supports 1 Gbps.

RJ45 connector

A clear plastic plug with eight gold-plated pins. Each pin cuts into one wire when crimped.

Why crimp your own?

You get the exact length you need, lower cost, and the skill to repair a damaged end.
